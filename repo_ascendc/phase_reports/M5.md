## Added structure

```text
ascendc_multi_turn/knowledge_v2/
├── experience.py                 # incident construction, diff, promotion and persistence
├── frontier.py                   # evaluation frontier classification and rollback selection
└── tests/test_m5.py              # M5 unit acceptance tests

<task>/.llm_state/
├── incidents/<incident-id>.json
├── experience_candidates/<experience-id>.json
└── frontiers/
    ├── manifest.json
    ├── source.json
    ├── compile.json
    ├── runtime.json
    ├── correctness.json
    └── performance.json
```

Frontier bundle files are materialized only after that frontier is reached. A deeper
candidate also establishes the implied shallower frontiers. The manifest records the
candidate attempt and performance score without modifying a knowledge snapshot.

## Changed behavior

- Bootstrap failures no longer unconditionally become the next edit baseline.
- A candidate that advances source/compile/runtime/correctness may become the stable
  repair frontier; a same-level failure restores the previously persisted candidate.
- A candidate rejected before source validation restores the deepest prior frontier,
  or an empty generated bundle when no safe frontier exists.
- Baseline/best selection remains correctness- and score-gated. Existing runs without
  frontier state seed it from a valid persisted best bundle on resume.
- Every trajectory round links its incident, frontier decision, and optional confirmed
  experience candidate.
- `CONFIRMED_EXPERIENCE` is a separate authority and cannot overwrite `OFFICIAL` facts.
